## Coconut husk biochar for dye removal

As an undergraduate researcher in BUET's Department of Chemical Engineering, I valorized coconut husk by pyrolysis into a nickel-activated carbon and tested it as an adsorbent for removing methyl orange, a common textile dye, from wastewater. The work grew into my first-author review of coconut-based porous materials for wastewater treatment and CO<sub>2</sub> capture in the *Journal of Environmental Management*, and later a co-authored review of adsorbents for pharmaceutical wastewater in *RSC Advances*.

## Water quality in transboundary rivers

At BUET's Institute of Water and Flood Management, I helped assess water and soil samples from two major transboundary river basins to identify pollution trends and seasonal variation. I co-authored a policy-oriented technical report that proposed a streamlined water-quality monitoring framework for adoption at the national level.
